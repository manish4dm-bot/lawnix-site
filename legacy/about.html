The Beginning

Where It All
Started

It started with a frustrating Saturday afternoon and a leveling rake that snapped in half. Our founder had just paid good money for a tool that lasted exactly one job. That moment changed everything.

We spent months researching what professional landscapers actually use — the tools they trust for their livelihood. The answer was always the same: heavy-gauge stainless steel, precision construction, and no shortcuts.

Lawnix was built to bring those professional-grade standards to every homeowner who takes pride in their lawn. Not watered-down versions. The real thing, at a price that makes sense.
