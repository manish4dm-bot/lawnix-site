Q Will this work on hard, compact clay soil or does it need to be loosened first?
A The RC-01 works best on moist or moderately compacted soil. For very hard, dry clay, we recommend watering the area the day before to soften the surface. The tine wheels will then work through it much more effectively. Many customers in clay-heavy areas lightly water first and have great results. — Lawnix™ Support
