Why level at all?

Fix the bumps

Mower wheels drop into low spots and scalp the high ones. A level surface mows evenly at any height.

Stop the puddles

Water pools in dips and drowns the grass. Filling them with sand lets the lawn drain.

Get the stripes

Striping, short cuts and reel mowers all need a flat canvas. This is how you build one.

Level a lawn in four passes

1

Mow low

Cut shorter than usual so the sand reaches the soil. Bag the clippings.

2

Spread

Dump topdressing sand or a sand and compost mix in piles across the low areas.

3

Level

Push and pull the rake in long passes. The plate drops sand into dips and skims the high spots.

4

Water in

Soak the lawn so the sand settles. Repeat in two to three weeks on stubborn spots.

Questions, answered

Which size should I buy?

The LX-30 suits most home lawns up to about a quarter acre. Pick the LX-48 for larger lawns, sports turf or if you level for other people. The wider plate means fewer passes but a heavier pull.

What material do I spread with it?

Dry masonry sand or a 70/30 sand and compost mix are the usual choices. Avoid wet or clumpy material; it will not flow through the plate.

Does it work on an uneven lawn with big dips?

Yes, in stages. Fill deep dips no more than half an inch per application so the grass can grow through, then repeat.

How long does assembly take?

Under five minutes. The two handle sections thread together and into the plate mount by hand.

Will the plate scratch or rust?

The plate is 304 stainless steel. It will pick up fine surface marks from sand, which is normal, and it does not rust.
