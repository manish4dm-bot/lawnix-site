Privacy

What Lawnix collects and why. Short version: only what it takes to ship your order and answer your email.

What we collect

When you buy, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your name, shipping address, email and payment details. We never see your full card number. When you join the email list we store your email address.

How we use it

To fulfil and ship orders, answer support requests, send order updates, and, if you opted in, occasional emails about Lawnix products. We do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

Payment processing (Stripe), order fulfilment and shipping partners, and the email service that sends our newsletter. Each receives only what it needs for that job.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or email hello@lawnixindustry.com to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you.

Cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies. Our checkout and email providers may set cookies on their own pages under their own policies.
